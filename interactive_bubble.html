Mountain Rescuse Incidents

In the bubble chart below, each bubble represents each incident happening on a specific date in a month (x-axis) and in a year (y-axis). The area of the bubbles is proportionate to the total hours spent in the rescues, that is calculated by number of rescuers times hours spent.

How to read this visualization

Color: Incident cause

Area of the circle: total number of hours of rescue
